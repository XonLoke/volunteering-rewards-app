Project title: Volunteering Rewards App
Domain: Application Development (includes mobile app)
Skill required: Web Application

Motivation and Benefits: 
- We can encourage Singaporeans to perform volunteering activities by rewarding their efforts. 
(e.g. performing a beach cleanup or taking the elderly for a morning walk)
- These volunteers earn points by scanning QR-Code after the volunteering activity. 
- When they have accumulated sufficient points, they can redeem these points for coupons from participating outlets. 
- Benefits of making this app is to encourage Singaporeans to get into the act of volunteering. 
- Distribution of rewards made via mobile devices would save time and manpower cost, and give the volunteer a wide range of rewards.

Project Details: 
- Volunteers scan the QR code markers to gain reward points. 
- When the user has accumulated sufficient points, he/she can redeem the coupon from a list of participating stores. 
- These coupons have a validity period and are issued in a fixed quantity. 
- When the user uses the coupon, he/she inform the cashier of the coupon’s 6-digit PIN. 
- The cashier enters the 6-digit PIN into the web app coupon verification. 
- Upon successful coupon verification, the cashier issues the item.
